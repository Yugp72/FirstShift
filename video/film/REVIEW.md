# FirstShift launch film: review notes

This is an ~89 s film at 1920×1080, 30 fps. It's generated by `build.py` (`python build.py`), which writes `index.html` and `compositions/*.html`. Edit the timing table in `build.py` rather than the HTML.

## Structure

| Part | Time | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Act 1, the problem** | 0–29.3 s | Statistics told as people (100 dots). Palo Alto 42.3% → Santa Clara County 55.2% → U.S. restaurant chefs 46% → 1 in 6 (WHO) → "One language. One format. One quiz." → a silent beat with the question → one dot becomes the FirstShift logo |
| **Act 2, the product** | 29.3–75.0 s | Real Playwright recordings of the live Enter deployment, with no mock-ups (see the list below) |
| **Act 3, the scale** | 75.0–89.0 s | ~7,000 languages (UNESCO) → "4 live today. Built to add the next." → end card |

The Act 2 recordings, in order:
1. Job posts
2. Language and learning setup (中文)
3. Lesson formats: video with captions, pictures, listen, read
4. Quiz, then "How sure are you?"
5. Mei's and Jorge's passports, with punch-ins on the real flags
6. Approve
7. The seal landing on Mei's phone (可以上班)

Every statistic carries its source line on screen. The sources were checked on Nov 18 (see `../HYPERFRAMES_SCRIPT.md` §2).

## Creative calls
- **One idea carries the opening:** each dot is a person. The film opens on a single dot, and a single dot becomes the logo, so the story is bookended.
- **The question beat has no voiceover,** and it's held for about 2.7 s on purpose.
- **Voiceover is Kokoro local TTS (`af_heart`).** The 中文 line uses `zf_xiaobei` and the Español line uses `ef_dora`. There is no Cantonese voice in Kokoro, so 粵語 appears on screen only.
- **No music bed.** HyperFrames' music retrieval needs a HeyGen sign-in. To add a track, drop it in as `assets/music.mp3` and add an `<audio>` element at about 0.14 volume.
- **Sound effects** come from the bundled HyperFrames library (Pixabay licence, see `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).

## Measured vs. looked at
- `hyperframes check` passes with no contract errors. The warnings kept on purpose:
  - `line_unread` on the question (the deliberate silent beat);
  - `text_below_floor` on the 26 px language words (texture);
  - a `first_frame_empty` false positive (a single dot is on screen at 0 s).
- Frames were reviewed at about 25 points across the film.
- **The audio was measured, not heard.**

## Weakest shots, given more time
- The phone UI is rendered at its native 390 px width, so phone text is small. Punch-ins on the quiz and the lesson tabs would help.
- The lesson's "listen" beat doesn't show the read-aloud highlight. A recording with audio permission would.
- A real music bed would carry the emotional opening far better than silence.
- A narrator recorded by the team, especially in Mandarin, Cantonese and Spanish, would beat TTS.

## Re-record the app clips
This needs `playwright-core@1.49.1` and Edge:
```bash
node record.mjs assets/rec
```
It records from the live deployment and resets the demo data at the end. Convert each clip to H.264 MP4 at 30 fps afterwards.
